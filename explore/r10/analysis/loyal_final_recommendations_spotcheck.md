## Spot check of the name extraction (by eye, only to find extraction bugs)

Done 2026-11-15, after `r10/analysis/loyal_final_recommendations.py` ran. This file is a copy of a project note with the title line and the script name changed. No category of any conversation was changed by judgement.

1. Fixed pick: the first two conversations (sorted by dialogue identifier) of each arm with 0, 1 and 2 or more named venues in the final agent message (12 messages): t0 MUL0015, MUL0027 (0); MUL0008, MUL0043 (1); MUL0079, MUL1085 (2+); t1 MUL0027, MUL0100 (0); MUL0008, MUL0015 (1); MUL0053, MUL0709 (2+). The extraction matched every database venue name written in these messages, and no name was wrong. The 0-name messages are closings ("You're welcome! Have a great trip to Cambridge."). One 2+ message lists eight venues in a table; one names a venue that the corpus prefix says the traveller booked elsewhere.
2. Mechanical search of all 0-name final messages for short forms of venue names (marriott, belfry, lensfield, holiday inn, university arms, gonville, ashley, lovell, cityroomz, lodge, b&b, guest house, bed and breakfast). Short forms that the rule misses: t0 MUL0188 and PMUL0479 ("the Huntingdon Marriott"), t1 PMUL3071 ("the Gonville"). The other 4 hits are the type word "guesthouse" (no venue). So the 0-name counts are too high by at most 2 (t0) and 1 (t1).
3. Effect on the decision: none. Even if every 0-name final message named exactly one venue, the share naming one would be (138 + 81) / 260 = 84.2% (t0) and (152 + 78) / 260 = 88.5% (t1), below the 90% of criterion F3a. Criterion F3b (cue share at most 5%) fails on its own: 22 of 138 (15.9%) and 15 of 152 (9.9%).
